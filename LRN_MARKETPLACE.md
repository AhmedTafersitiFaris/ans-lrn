# LRN Marketplace Model

A future marketplace/API may let a user search and select an LRN, inspect its exact version and governance/commercial status, creator attribution, available evidence, negative evidence, known failures, audit history, and applicable pricing before use or purchase.

The catalog shows reviewable data, not statements of truth. It clearly distinguishes governance state, commercial state, evidence availability, limits, and price terms. A listing cannot activate, validate, or otherwise alter an LRN.

Catalog eligibility is checked against the linked governance record. Suspended or retired records may be discoverable for history/audit only, not operational sale. Access control and pricing are future implementation concerns.
