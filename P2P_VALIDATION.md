# Peer-to-Peer Validation

Peer-to-peer validation distributes review; it does not distribute permanent trust. A creator submits evidence, an independent validator makes a traceable decision, and an independent auditor can review the artifact or the validator's process. A counter-audit may challenge any prior result.

Independence means no self-validation and disclosure of material conflicts. Participants retain the complete chain of proposal, evidence, tests, decision, dissent, and later status changes. Consensus cannot override a recorded safety concern without a new, auditable governance decision.

Review compensation is outcome-neutral: `review assigned → work performed → review submitted → compensation`. Neither approval nor rejection is a payment condition. Any direct financial interest in a commercial outcome is disclosed, recorded, and mitigated or the reviewer recuses themself.
