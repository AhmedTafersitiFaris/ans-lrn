# Economic Policy v0.1

DRAFT FOR REVIEW - NOT ADOPTED
Prepared: 2026-10-13.

This draft develops ECONOMIC_MODEL.md. It is not a price schedule, license, financial contract or payment implementation.

No percentage, tariff, payout threshold or production entitlement is adopted. Simulation allocations remain non-policy fixtures.

## Existing boundaries

ECONOMIC STATE != GOVERNANCE STATE

Payment cannot grant validation, activation, deployment permission or execution authority. Price, revenue and popularity do not establish correctness.

Creators cannot purchase favorable validation or suppress negative evidence. Equivalent documented review work has equivalent compensation regardless of APPROVE or REJECT.

SIMULATION creates no payable balance, invoice or payout. REAL operational commercial usage requires eligibility of the exact version under adopted policy. Suspended and retired versions cannot be sold for operational use.

Regulatory and data-processing authorization remain separate.

## Proposed price decomposition

Separate operational delivery costs from remuneration for knowledge and services:

1. Resources: compute, external providers, network, storage, security and reliability.
2. Creator knowledge and maintenance remuneration.
3. Platform catalog, distribution, metering and support.
4. Governance and audit funding.
5. Review funding when explicitly applicable.

Declare whether each component is included, pass-through, subsidized or separately funded. Do not double-charge resource costs.

Computational profiles use simulation resource units, not calibrated monetary costs. Production conversion needs documented, versioned rates.

Expose price unit, currency, rounding, resources, validity window and exact version before use. Insufficient authorized budget requires rejection or review, not silent overdraft.

## Accounting

Preserve integer minor units and exact reconciliation:

gross = resource cost + creator allocation + platform allocation + governance allocation + review-pool allocation + remaining balance

This simulation decomposition is not a complete production ledger. Taxes, fees, refunds, discounts, subsidies, reserves and chargebacks need explicit policy and schema review before REAL billing.

Version economic terms independently from the LRN. Do not silently reprice historical events. Corrections require traceable adjustments.

## Remuneration and review funding

Creation and maintenance may be remunerated through authorized metered usage under versioned terms. Royalties do not prove correctness.

Platform remuneration must not duplicate resource charges.

Initial validation, periodic revalidation, audit and counter-audit must be funded as documented work independently of outcome. Low revenue cannot exempt an LRN from necessary review.

Creator funding, platform subsidy, consumer funding and pooled funding are options to evaluate, not adopted decisions.

Declare funding sources and conflicts. Submitters cannot control favorable reviewer selection or outcomes. Additional compensation requires documented additional work, not approval.

## Free/open and commercial versions

Separate licensing, resource cost and governance. Free/open LRNs may have execution costs; zero-price offers must identify who funds execution and review.

Open access grants no unrestricted data-processing or deployment permission.

Commercial listings identify exact version, scope, attribution, license, pricing unit and eligibility. No exclusivity, sublicensing or license change is adopted without the rights holder's explicit decision.

## Metering and privacy

Distinguish retrieval, invocation, execution and output. Decide billable stages before implementation and prevent duplicate charging of one logical operation.

Specify idempotency, retries, failures, partial runs, duplicate delivery, cancellation and refunds. A success flag alone does not prove a billable outcome.

Keep telemetry minimal and access-controlled. Public GitHub contains simulations and sanitized specifications, not identifiable production usage or financial/customer records.

Do not store prompts, outputs, secrets or assisted-person data in metadata. Pseudonymous identifiers still require privacy and retention assessment.

## Suspension and disputes

Suspension or retirement stops new operational sales. Historical remuneration and refunds require explicit policy; suspension does not automatically erase accounting history.

Define independent disputes for metering, performance, licensing and review conflicts. Economic disputes cannot themselves change governance status.

Until explicitly resolved and adopted, use SIMULATION only. This draft authorizes no real payments, Stripe, banking, crypto, tokens or production billing.

## Future acceptance criteria

Prices and allocations reference adopted policy and exact LRN versions. Economic changes cannot grant governance or deployment authority. Ineligible REAL events are rejected; SIMULATION cannot create payable balances.

Equivalent review compensation is outcome-independent. Integer accounting reconciles; adjustments are traceable; retries cannot double-charge.

Restricted deployment or data permission blocks execution regardless of payment. Sensitive telemetry cannot enter the public/P2P registry.

## Adoption record

Policy owner/process, adoption date, effective version, monetary terms and approval reference: not defined/adopted.

Policy adoption and schema/runtime implementation are separate work. Merging this draft does not authorize real-money behavior.
